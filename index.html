What I build

Focused tools that do one job well and fit into the systems your team already relies on.

CRM integrations

Two-way sync of contacts, companies, deals and tickets between your CRM and external systems, built on official APIs and OAuth.

Workflow automation

Custom workflow actions, webhooks and background jobs that remove repetitive data entry and keep records consistent.

CRM extensions

App cards and settings pages that bring outside data and actions right into the CRM record your team is working on.

SaaS products

Multi-tenant cloud apps with secure authentication, billing-ready architecture and monitoring from day one.

How I work

A short, predictable path from idea to a working integration.

Discover

Map the systems, data and workflows involved, and agree on what success looks like.

Design

Define the data model, sync rules and the minimum set of permissions the app needs.

Build & test

Implement against official APIs, test in sandbox accounts and handle edge cases and rate limits.

Launch & support

Ship with clear setup documentation, monitor usage and keep the app current as APIs evolve.

Principles

Every app I ship follows the same ground rules.

  • Least privilegeApps request only the permissions they actually use.
  • Privacy firstCustomer data is used only to provide the service, never sold or shared.
  • Secure by defaultOAuth, encrypted connections and tokens stored securely.
  • Clear documentationSetup, usage and uninstall steps written for real users.
