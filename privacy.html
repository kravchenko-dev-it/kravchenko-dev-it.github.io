Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pavlo Kravchenko ("I", "me", "my") collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit this website or contact me. Individual apps I publish may have an additional, app-specific privacy notice that describes the data that app processes; where it does, that notice applies together with this policy.

1. Data I collect

  • Information you send me. If you email me, I receive your email address, name and anything you include in your message.
  • Technical data. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address for security and operational purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

2. How I use data

  • To reply to your questions and support requests.
  • To provide, maintain and improve the apps and services you use.
  • To meet legal obligations.

I do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

3. Data accessed by my apps

When you install one of my apps and connect it to a third-party platform (for example, a CRM such as HubSpot), the app accesses only the data covered by the permissions you approve during installation. That data is used solely to provide the app's functionality to you, is not sold or shared with third parties for their own purposes, and is deleted or returned when you uninstall the app and request deletion. The specific data each app accesses is listed in its documentation.

4. Legal basis

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, I process personal data on the basis of performing a contract with you, my legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and running my services, or your consent where required.

5. Sharing

I share personal data only with service providers that help me operate my services (such as hosting and email providers), under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations, or when required by law.

6. Retention

I keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Correspondence is generally kept for up to two years after our last contact, unless a longer period is required by law.

7. Security

I use encrypted connections, access controls and secure storage of credentials and tokens to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but I take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, email me at pavlo.kravchenko.it@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children

My website and apps are intended for businesses and are not directed at children under 16.

10. Changes

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
